Our approach

Coffee is more than a drink.

At miau coffee, every cup starts long before it reaches your table — with specialty-grade beans chosen for their story, their origin and the way they taste when they're treated with care.

We roast relationships with the farms we buy from as carefully as we roast the beans themselves, then brew each drink by hand using methods chosen to bring out what makes that coffee worth drinking slowly.

  • Specialty-grade beans, carefully selected origins
  • Professional brewing & genuine craftsmanship
  • An atmosphere built for community & quality time
