Cookie Policy

miau coffee's website uses a small number of cookies to make the site work well and to understand how it's used.

Essential cookies

These are required for basic site functionality, such as remembering your menu filter selection or that you've closed a banner. The site cannot function properly without them.

We may use anonymous analytics cookies to understand which pages are popular and how visitors move through the site, so we can keep improving it.

Managing cookies

Most browsers let you control cookies through their settings. You can choose to block or delete cookies, though some parts of the site may not work as intended if you do.
